Featured Titles

Beyond the First Betrayal
Romantic Suspense / Political Conspiracy Thriller

Eva Reyes notices what everyone else misses. When a routine audit reveals thirty‑five stolen minutes in a government log, she pulls the thread no one else would — and uncovers a cover‑up that reaches the highest office in the country. The erased woman at the center of it, the program meant to stay buried, and the truth about Eva’s own father collide in a courtroom where someone is buying a verdict. As the conspiracy closes in and a slow‑burn romance with the one colleague she trusts finally surfaces, Eva must decide how much of her life she’s willing to risk to finish what she started. Some betrayals are personal. This one is national.

Beyond the Chambers
Legal Thriller / Romantic Suspense

Twenty‑three years ago, Judge Rowan Calder loved a woman on a lake‑house dock and let her walk away. Now Marina Vale is sitting at the defense table in his courtroom, charged with manslaughter, and he is the only man alive who can save her. He buries evidence. He compromises a juror. It should be enough — until a sharp‑eyed prosecutor and a relentless investigator start pulling at the threads of his lie. As the conspiracy protecting them both begins to unravel, Calder must decide how much of his life he’s willing to burn down for a woman he hasn’t spoken to in over a decade, and has never stopped loving. Some debts take twenty‑three years to come due. This one is about to collect.

Beyond the Flight Plan
Romantic Suspense / Corporate Conspiracy

Captain Julian hasn’t looked at his first officer in six months — until one hotel bar changes everything. What begins as an affair neither of them planned becomes a reckoning: a falsified safety audit, a blackmailer with a decade of leverage, and a family secret Sarah spent eleven years outrunning. As the conspiracy tightens around them and Julian’s marriage collapses under its own weight, both of them must decide what’s left worth saving. Some affairs ground everything. Some truths do too.

About VJ Webster

VJ Webster writes suspense romance that blends danger, desire and emotional heat into stories that grip from the first page. His characters face impossible choices, hidden truths and consequences that tighten around them as passion rises. Every novel pulls readers into a world where trust is fragile, secrets ignite conflict and love becomes the most dangerous force of all.
