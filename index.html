Beyond the First Betrayal
Romantic Suspense / Political Conspiracy Thriller

Eva Reyes notices what everyone else misses. When a routine audit reveals thirty five stolen minutes in a government log, she pulls the thread no one else would and uncovers a cover up that reaches the highest office in the country. The erased woman at the center of it, the program meant to stay buried, and the truth about Eva’s own father collide in a courtroom where someone is buying a verdict. As the conspiracy closes in and a slow burn romance with the one colleague she trusts finally surfaces, Eva must decide how much of her life she is willing to risk to finish what she started. Some betrayals are personal. This one is national.

Beyond the Chambers
Legal Thriller / Romantic Suspense

Twenty three years ago, Judge Rowan Calder loved a woman on a lake house dock and let her walk away. Now Marina Vale is sitting at the defense table in his courtroom, charged with manslaughter, and he is the only man alive who can save her. He buries evidence. He compromises a juror. It should be enough until a sharp eyed prosecutor and a relentless investigator start pulling at the threads of his lie. As the conspiracy protecting them both begins to unravel, Calder must decide how much of his life he is willing to burn down for a woman he has not spoken to in over a decade and has never stopped loving. Some debts take twenty three years to come due. This one is about to collect.
